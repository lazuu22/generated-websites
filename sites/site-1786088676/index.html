About the Game

Grand City Roleplay is a luxury roleplay game that offers a realistic roleplay system, jobs and businesses, police and criminal factions, houses and vehicles, voice and text chat, events and rewards, economy system, daily missions, and custom characters.

Realistic Roleplay System

Experience a realistic roleplay system that allows you to create your own character and interact with others in the game.

Jobs and Businesses

Take on various jobs and businesses to earn money and build your career in the game.

Police and Criminal Factions

Join the police force or become a member of a criminal faction to experience the thrill of the game.

Houses and Vehicles

Buy and customize your own houses and vehicles to show off your wealth and status in the game.

Voice and Text Chat

Communicate with other players using voice and text chat to build relationships and alliances in the game.

Events and Rewards

Participate in events and complete missions to earn rewards and bonuses in the game.

Economy System

Manage your finances and build your wealth in the game using the economy system.

Daily Missions

Complete daily missions to earn rewards and bonuses in the game.

Custom Characters

Create your own custom characters to play as in the game.
